Visual system 1.0 · No logo in this document

We understand the complexity. You don’t have to.

Zephrok is an AI-native operating system for running an online business. This is the visual language around the product — colour, type, space, records, and behaviour. It is a working system, not a moodboard.

Trustworthy guardian · Simple · Modern · Calm · Confident
Orange is a verb. Numbers are identity. AI is a record of work.

00 · How to use

A designer who has never spoken to us should be able to ship from this.

Every major rule is written as Rule → Reason → Correct → Incorrect. If a decision is not here, default to less: fewer colours, fewer radii, fewer cards, less motion. Do not design a logo here. Set the word Zephrok in Source Sans 3 semibold.

Start here

Read the four principles. Then colour, then type. Applications at the end show the system under load — dashboard, ledger, AI operations, marketing.

Tokens

tokens/tokens.css is canonical. tokens/shadcn-theme.css is the tweakcn-shaped export for product UI. Do not restyle shadcn back to default radius and cool gray.

Paper

This file is Paper-native HTML/CSS. Each section is an artboard. Import frames into Paper with the same tokens; do not restyle in the canvas.

01 · Position

An intelligent operating system for serious businesses.

Not another SaaS tile. Not a futuristic AI lab. Zephrok holds money, customers, inventory, and operations in one place. The brand has to feel like control without complexity — capable, quiet, and specific.

Is

  • A back-office OS for shops that currently live in six tools
  • A record of what happened, why, and what to do next
  • A policy-gated system: draft, apply, or refuse — never “the model decided”
  • Flexible later for retail, agencies, travel, restaurants

Is not

  • A chatbot with a dashboard glued on
  • A neon AI startup, a bank clone, or a generic CRM
  • Ecommerce-coded so hard it cannot leave shops
  • A place that hides a failed payment behind friendliness

02 · Personality → design

The product’s character is the layout, not a tone of voice overlay.

Rule

Translate personality into constraints, not adjectives on a poster.

Reason: “Calm” that is only a word becomes a lavender gradient. Constraints can be checked in review.

03 · Four principles

These four things should still read as Zephrok if the name is removed.

If a layout could belong to any AI/SaaS company, it is not done. Do not add a fifth principle. Do not invent a mascot shape to “own” the brand.

01

The Spine

A 3px Ember mark shows the current context: nav item, selected row, live event, focused section. It is a status indicator, not a stripe decoration. One spine per context. Never a 3px rule on every card.

02

Warm Ledger

Canvas is warm paper. Type is ink. Rules are hairline. Shadows almost never. The product should feel like a well-kept record, not a cool-gray SaaS dashboard or a night-mode sci-fi console.

03

One Orange Verb

In any view, Ember is allowed on the spine, the focus ring, and one primary action. That is it. Orange is what you do next — not a theme, not a fill, not an illustration colour.

04

The Record

Information is laid out as a ledger: when / who on the left, what happened in the middle, numbers on the right. Tables, activity, AI actions, and invoices share this skeleton. AI work appears in the same record as human work.

04 · Colour

Warm paper, ink, and one ember. Everything else is status.

Rule

Build every screen from Paper, Surface, Ink, Line, then Ember, then one status colour if the state needs it.

Reason: a small, warm system is recognisable. A rainbow of “brand supporting colours” is not a brand. It is a default palette.

05 · Orange behaviour

Ember creates recognition. Fatigue is a brand failure.

Rule

Never set Ember as small text on Paper. Use Ember Strong for any orange fill that contains white text. Cap Ember to spine + focus + one verb.

Reason: #E24A1B is 3.9:1 on white — fine as a 3px mark and a chart series, not as 14px copy. #BE3A0E is 5.5:1 on white — that is the button.

07 · Data visualisation

Charts explain a record. They are not brand decoration.

Rule

Use the six categorical colours in order. Ember is series 1 only when it is the series the user should watch. Axes, grid, and labels are Ink 3 and Line — never coloured.

Reason: rainbow dashboards look like every analytics template. A ledger with one emphasised series looks like Zephrok.

08 · Typography

Three voices. One system. Not a fashionable pairing.

Source Serif 4 speaks for the business in public. Source Sans 3 does the work. IBM Plex Mono is the machine: IDs, timestamps, policy names. Money never goes in mono — it stays in Source Sans with tabular figures.

Rule

Weights are 400, 500, 600 only. No 700 in product. No uppercase labels with 0.08em tracking — that is generic SaaS chrome.

Reason: 700 plus tracked caps is how every shadcn dashboard shouts. We already have hierarchy from size and the spine.

09 · Numbers

If a table of numbers does not look like Zephrok, the identity has failed.

Rule

Always font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums. Right-align quantities and money. Keep the currency symbol in the same tabular run. Negative values use Error colour, not parentheses and a minus.

Reason: proportional figures jitter in a column of invoices. Jitter reads as untrustworthy in finance.

10 · Iconography

Icons name a thing. They do not furnish a layout.

Rule

24×24 grid, 1.5px stroke, round caps, 1.5px corners, optical alignment. Inactive = Ink 3. Active = Ember Strong stroke, no fill except a 20% Ember Tint if the icon is the only selected control.

Reason: filled candy icons and 2.5px “friendly” strokes belong to consumer apps. We need density in nav and tables.

No decorative icons beside headings “to add interest.” If removing the icon loses no information, remove it.

11 · Space & grid

4px is the unit. Product is dense. Marketing can breathe. They stay related.

Rule

Product spacing: 8, 12, 16, 24. Marketing spacing: 24, 40, 64, 96. Gutters 24. Marketing max width 1200. Prose 720. Never mix a 13px gap in because it “looked better.”

Reason: a startup team cannot keep a 12-step spacing scale consistent. This one is short enough to memorise.

4 8 12 16 24 32 40 48 64 — stop. 20 and 80 exist in tokens for optical exceptions only.

12 · Layout

Do not put everything in a card. A card is a unit of work.

Rule

Use a card only when the object is independently actionable: an invoice, a conversation, a SKU, a draft. Lists, tables, page titles, and filters sit on Paper with hairlines.

Reason: card-on-card dashboards are the generic SaaS look. A ledger on paper is ours.

Product

Ink strip on top (OS). Nav with spine. Main column is the record. Detail pane on the right when a row is selected — not a modal if the user is comparing. Radius 4 on controls, 6 on the detail pane, 0 on tables.

Marketing

Serif display, spine beside the headline, real product (the ledger) as the image, one Ember Strong CTA. No fake glassy UI, no 3D OS windows floating in space, no stock founders.

13 · Graphic language

If it would still work as a blob, a grid of dots, or a neural net, delete it.

The recognisable Zephrok picture is: warm sheet, ink type, a 3px ember spine, a ledger of events, tabular numbers on the right. That scene should be repeatable on a website, a slide, a social card, and the product itself.

Shape

Rectangles with 4–6px corners. Square spine ticks. No circles as brand marks. No hexagons. No isometric cities.

Line

1px Line for structure. 3px Ember for “now.” That’s the whole line language.

Composition

Left-weighted. Spine or time column anchors the left. Numbers fall to the right. Headlines sit next to the spine, not floating in a field of white.

14 · Imagery

Photograph the work, or don’t photograph.

Rule

Prefer the live product (ledgers, drafts, conversations) as the image. If you use photography: real shops, real goods, real paper and hands at work, cropped tight, warm colour match to Paper (#F6F3EE grade), no teal-orange cinematic grade, no laptop-gaze portraits, no robots.

Reason: stock entrepreneurship is indistinguishable. A cropped photograph of packed orders next to a Zephrok ledger is specific and expandable to restaurants or travel later — it is operations, not “ecommerce neon.”

Treatment

  • Exposure slightly high, shadows warm
  • Saturation pulled toward umber and ember, never cyan
  • Fine grain allowed; HDR and glow not allowed
  • Overlay type in Ink or Surface only
  • Social/cards: product crop + one line of serif + spine

Never

  • People staring at laptops / fake standups
  • Hands on glass dashboards
  • Brains, neurons, holograms, circuits
  • Generated “AI founders” or sparkle dust
  • Unrelated fashion photography as “premium”

15 · Motion

Fast, precise, quiet. Motion is a status change.

Rule

120ms micro (hover, focus). 180ms state (expand, tab, row select). 240ms panel. Easing cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1). No bounce. No spring. AI processing is a 3px Ember filling the spine from top to bottom, then it becomes a record.

Reason: bounce and particles say “consumer toy.” A spine that fills says “the system is working on this row.”

16 · AI visual language

Intelligence is how the system behaves, not what it is decorated with.

Zephrok is AI-native and policy-gated. The model never “decides money.” Draft, apply, and forbidden are visible states. Attribution is System or a named human, in mono. Copy is: what happened → why → what the user can do next.

Rule

No robots, brains, neural nets, sparkles, purple gradients, holograms, circuit boards, or “magic” particles. No separate “AI bubble” style. System events use the same Record as human events.

Reason: those symbols are how every AI startup looks in 2024–26. Zephrok should look like an operating system that happens to be fast.

17 · UI principles

Product UI is the brand at 14px.

Do

  • Ink OS strip, then Paper workspace
  • One Ember Strong button per view
  • Filters as text + hairline, not chip clouds
  • Forms: label above, 6px gap, 36px control
  • Destructive actions are Error, never Ember
  • Empty states explain what to do next, 2 lines max

Don’t

  • Glassmorphism, heavy shadows, 16px radii
  • Gradient buttons, neon focus, animated borders
  • Fake metrics to make a dashboard look “alive”
  • Onboarding confetti, empty-state illustrations of rockets
  • Using Ember for destructive money-out
  • Hiding density behind more cards

Approve is the one verb. Reject is Error. Ember is not used for both.

18 · Do / Don’t

A short list a tired designer can use at 1am.

Do use Paper + Ink + one Ember verb

Don’t theme the whole UI orange

Do show what / why / next on events

Don’t write “Oops” around money

Do tabular numbers, right aligned

Don’t put amounts in colourful badges

Do mark the current row with a spine

Don’t put a spine on every module

Do keep AI in the same ledger

Don’t invent an AI mascot or glow

Do 4px / 6px radius, no shadow

Don’t default shadcn 8px + drop shadow

Do Source Serif only in public/marketing

Don’t serif inside dense tables

Do draft = ochre, posted = forest

Don’t mint green / hot red traffic lights

19 · Applications

The same system, under load.

Four working surfaces. They share tokens, type, spine, and the Record. Open them. If they feel like four different products, the system broke.

Operations OS Finance ledger AI activity Marketing

Also: presentations use the marketing type scale and a left spine. Social cards are a cropped Record + one serif line. Apparel, if any, is Ink on Paper or Paper on Ink — Ember as a 3px mark only.
