# Bittokx Logo Design

## Visual philosophy: Precision Orbit

The Bittokx identity is built from the language of digital value moving through a secure system. The mark uses a token-like outer ring, a constructed `B`, and a diagonal exchange path to suggest momentum, conversion, and custody without becoming literal or decorative. It should feel engineered, premium, and trustworthy.

The form is intentionally compact: the circle gives the brand a strong app-icon silhouette, while the internal `B` remains legible at small sizes. The diagonal path is the quiet conceptual detail. It reads as a transaction route, a swap motion, and a signal passing through the brand's core.

The palette balances institutional confidence with market energy. Deep navy anchors the logo, electric cyan signals technology and speed, and warm amber brings token value and optimism. The wordmark is heavy, close-set, and geometric to match the density of the icon.

## Usage notes

- Use the full lockup for websites, pitch decks, headers, and formal brand moments.
- Use the standalone mark for app icons, favicons, social avatars, watermarks, and exchange dashboard UI.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least half the icon diameter.
- Avoid placing the mark on busy photography or low-contrast gradients.
- Prefer the SVG files for production; export raster sizes from them as needed.
