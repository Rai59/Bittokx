# Bittokx Logo

## The idea

The mark is a lowercase **b** built from exactly two primitive shapes:

- a vertical **bar** — the digit `1`
- a **circle** — the digit `0`

`1` and `0` are binary: the **bit**. The circle is also the universal shape of a
**token** (a coin). Together they spell the first letter of Bittokx. One concept,
two shapes, zero decoration. The small gap between the bar and the circle is the
mark's single distinctive detail: the token stands alone, ready to move.

## Design principles applied

- **Simplicity** — the entire mark is one rectangle and one circle. Nothing else.
- **Memorability** — "the logo is literally a 1 and a 0" is a story people can
  retell in one sentence, which is what makes a mark stick.
- **Timelessness** — no gradients, no effects, no trend-driven styling. Pure
  geometry in black and white, in the tradition of marks like Audi's rings.
- **Versatility** — designed in black first. It works as a favicon at 16 px, as a
  single-color print, embossed, reversed on black, or animated (the token can
  roll, orbit, or snap into the bar).
- **Relevance** — bar + coin reads as fintech/crypto without resorting to
  clichés such as rockets, arrows, or hexagons.

The wordmark is not a typeset font. Every letter is constructed from the same
geometric grid as the mark (16-unit strokes, 24-unit circle radius), so the
wordmark and mark share DNA and the SVG renders identically everywhere with no
font dependency.

## Color

Black `#000000` on light surfaces, white `#FFFFFF` on dark surfaces. The
identity is deliberately monochrome; if a brand accent is ever needed, apply it
to the circle only, never to both shapes.

## Usage rules

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of its bar.
- Do not outline, shadow, rotate, or add gradients to the mark.
- Do not close the gap between the bar and the circle.
- On photography or color, use whichever of black/white has more contrast.
