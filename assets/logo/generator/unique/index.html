Four marks that do not look like two circles.

Eclipse is clear. These four are built to be remembered: a slot, a letter, a bite, a lock. Same company — an AI-native OS that connects and automates e-commerce — different silhouette.

Keybit

A whole with a bit cut as a keyway. The OS is the key to the store — one object, one mechanical twist.

Operator B

A custom B whose counters are bits. Ownable like a lettermark, not a diagram of connection.

Bit OS

The operating system as a rounded square — Apple’s bite, on the product itself.

Crosslock

The X in Bittokx. One bar solid, one open — business and AI locked. Not a close icon.
